Hello there, my name's Cory! I'm a current senior attending the Journalism program at the University of Missouri in Columbia (MIZZOU!) If there's one thing I'd want you to know about me, it's that I love cars... enough to the point to where it was the reason I even decided to come here to the University in the first place! I'm aiming to pursue a career in automotive journalism because of just how much I love the amazing world of the automobile. For me, it's more than just a niche hobby... it's a genuine way of life.

    A little about myself!

  • Age: 21 years old
  • Mizzou Journalism Student (Class of 2027)
  • Photographed/wrote for Vox Magazine and the Columbia Missourian
  • Owner of a Cadillac Seville STS (the image above) and car enthusiast for life!

    What got me into cars?

  1. Getting to sit behind the wheel of classic cars as a child
  2. Riding around in the backseat spotting cool cars out the window
  3. Watching automotive journalists review new vehicles online
  4. The fact that the overall art of driving feels very relaxing
