PhoDoc 1 Profile Story- Andrew Brandt

  • On October 19, 2025, hundreds of sparks fly as Andrew Brandt sharpens a lawn mower blade on a grinder blade at the Brandt Chainsaw shop in Chamois, Mo.
  • Andrew Brandt sits inside of an old shed filled with old chainsaws on the Brandt Chainsaw property in Chamois, Mo. on October 19, 2025. Ever since he was 11 years old, Brandt received training by his father Dennis to learn how to work in the family business. “I call it my dad’s professional lecture,” says Andrew. “It’s where he tells me all the facts about the mowers and chainsaws, and it’s where I soaked in the information.”
  • In Chamois, Mo. on October 19, 2025, Andrew Brandt shares a tender moment with Twix, an Australian Shepherd and one of three dogs owned by the family. The other two dogs are named Coco and Bella. When not working down at the shop, Brandt will often spend most of his time with Twix.”We got her frommy uncle Matt, whose dogs had 8 puppies,” explains Andrew. “Twix became our farm and shop friend dog just like Coco."
  • After properly inspecting and sharpening a chainsaw blade on a Stihl chainsaw, Andrew Brandt tests it on a pile of wood behind the Brandt Chainsaw property in Chamois, Mo. on October 19, 2025.

Columbia Missourian- MU Formula SAE Team

  • From left, Justin Bowers and Jack Fels converse about race car 115 on April 29 inside the Mizzou Racing workshop at Lafferre Hall. “Right now we’re fitting a pressure sensor onto our crankcase of the engine so we can measure the pressure inside the crankcase,” Bowers said. “We’re going to attempt to pull some minor vacuum on that to improve our engine efficiency and hopefully make a little more power as a result.”
  • Leah Schatz works on electrical components of the battery of one of the battery-powered race cars April 29 inside the Mizzou Racing workshop at Lafferre Hall. The car’s battery is split into five different segments, which the team has named after characters from Scooby Doo.
  • Alex Kunz prepares to weld together the exhaust header of one of the race car’s engines April 27 at Lafferre Hall. “I like creating things and then seeing how hard we worked on it and how much time and effort we put into it,” Kunz said. “It’s nice to see it drive and be the best car that we’ve built so far.”
