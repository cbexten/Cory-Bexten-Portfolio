Columbia Missourian: "Groundbreaking held for new MU radioisotope research center"

University of Missouri System leadership and employees of the MU Research Reactor Center gathered on Thursday to celebrate the groundbreaking of a new 31,250-square-foot Radioisotope Science Center at Discovery Ridge Research Park. The U.S. Department of Energy and the state of Missouri have each invested $20 million into the development of the facility.
